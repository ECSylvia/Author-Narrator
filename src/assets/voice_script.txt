VOXAUTHOR VOICE SAMPLE SCRIPT v0.1
Read in your normal speaking voice. Stay relaxed.
If you make a mistake, pause, then start the sentence again.
Try to record in a quiet room, about 6–10 inches from the mic.

PART 1: Warm-up (natural tone)
1) My name is ________. Today I’m recording a voice sample for VoxAuthor.
2) I will read steadily, clearly, and at a comfortable pace.
3) I’m going to speak like I would when telling a good story to a friend.

PART 2: Everyday speech (vowels and consonants in context)
4) The morning was cool, and the light through the window looked soft and clean.
5) I walked down the hall, picked up my keys, and checked the lock twice.
6) A small cup of coffee can change the whole rhythm of a day.
7) The older I get, the more I notice the quiet details people miss.
8) I didn’t rush. I let the moment settle before I decided what to do next.

PART 3: Crisp consonants and tricky blends
Read clearly but do not over-enunciate.
9) Crisp, clean, clear speech can still sound warm and human.
10) Please bring the blue backpack before you leave.
11) The streetlight flickered, then steadied, then flickered again.
12) The thick cloth strap slipped from my shoulder.
13) I asked for three fresh slices of peach pie.

PART 4: S and Sh, Ch and J, F and V
14) She sells simple seashell souvenirs by the seashore shop.
15) I chose the cheaper chair, but it felt just fine.
16) The judge adjusted the jacket and checked the page again.
17) Five vivid photos showed the view from the very top.
18) I felt the finish on the frame and found a few flaws.

PART 5: R and L, W and Y (common model pain points)
19) Rory rarely runs late, but today he really had to hurry.
20) Lily rolled the letter and placed it in the blue folder.
21) We were waiting while the wind whistled through the wide doorway.
22) Yesterday I knew you would say yes, even if you tried to hide it.

PART 6: Plosives and breath control (P, B, T, D, K, G)
Keep your volume steady and do not pop the mic.
23) The paper packet was packed tight and placed on the porch.
24) A bright bird bounced between branches and disappeared.
25) Take the time to tell the truth, even when it is difficult.
26) The door clicked closed, and the sound carried down the dark hallway.
27) I kept going, because quitting would have been easier than finishing.

PART 7: Long sentences and short sentences (rhythm range)
28) When I finally reached the edge of town, the noise faded behind me, and I could hear my own footsteps again.
29) I stopped.
30) I listened.
31) Nothing moved.
32) Then everything did.

PART 8: Emotion range (controlled, not theatrical)
